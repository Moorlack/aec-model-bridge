---
name: design-building
description: Design a complete Revit building through AEC Model Bridge, from brief through verified documentation.
---

For the user's building brief, gather only missing geometry-critical facts: use, footprint and units, floor count/heights, rooms, structural system, and deliverables. Confirm connection, inspect existing datum and loaded types, then create in order: levels/grids; structure; envelope/partitions; openings; floors/roof; rooms/MEP; views, annotations, schedules, sheets, and export. Verify each phase. Do not stop for permission after every wall when the design scope is explicit; ask one question only when an unresolved choice materially changes the model.
